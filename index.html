Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy — Andavar Ambulance Service

Last Updated: July 2026  |  Effective Date: July 2026

1. Who We Are

Andavar Ambulance Service ("we", "our", "us") provides 24/7 emergency medical transport and hospital transfer services across Tamil Nadu, based in Chennai. We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with applicable Indian laws.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our website or contact us, we may collect:

  • Personal Information: Name, mobile number, and location details you voluntarily provide when filling our booking or enquiry form.
  • Location Data: Pickup and drop locations entered in our booking form to calculate distance estimates. This data is processed to generate your booking summary and sent via WhatsApp to our team.
  • Usage Data: Basic browser and device information collected automatically when you visit our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm ambulance bookings via WhatsApp.
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To improve our website and service.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • WhatsApp: Booking and enquiry details are transmitted via WhatsApp to coordinate your ambulance.
  • Location Suggestions: Pickup/drop location suggestions and approximate distance are calculated using a built-in list of cities and towns in Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala and Andhra Pradesh — no location data is sent to any external mapping service during this step.
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law or to protect public safety.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at 95660 09943.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of our site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

For any privacy concerns or questions, contact us at:
